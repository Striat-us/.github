> **Current AI predicts tokens. Striatus is being built to predict human behavior.**

Striatus is a decision intelligence platform in development. It combines **causal reasoning**, **cognitive neuroscience**, and **neuro-symbolic AI** to model how people make decisions when they're under pressure, facing uncertainty, or juggling competing constraints.

The name comes from the **striatum**, the brain region at the center of decision-making, reward processing, and goal-directed behavior. It's where cognition meets action.

## What we're building

| Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🧭 **Causal Decision Modeling** | Maps the causal structure behind decisions: what happened, why it happened, and what would have happened under different conditions. |
| 🧠 **Cognitive Digital Twins** | Builds behavioral models of individuals, teams, and organizations from scientific literature and real-world decision data. |
| 🔮 **Predictive Simulation** | Runs forward-looking scenarios to test how people and organizations are likely to respond before anyone commits to an action. |
| 🏗️ **Choice Architecture Optimization** | Identifies the interventions and communications most likely to produce better decision outcomes. |

## Principles

- **Causation, not correlation.** We explain *why*, not just *what*.
- **Sovereign-first.** The platform is designed for on-premise and air-gapped deployment.
- **Humans stay in charge.** Striatus is a scientific mirror for decision-makers, not a replacement for human judgment.
